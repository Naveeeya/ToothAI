## The mark

A tooth outline (4.5-unit stroke on a 64-unit grid) with a four-point spark at its top right. The spark
is separated from the tooth by a gap; in these files the gap is a mask, so the mark works on any
background. The wordmark is **Tooth** + **AI** in Plus Jakarta Sans ExtraBold, tracked −4 %, with "AI"
in Spark Blue (Spark Light on dark).

- **Clear space:** equal to the spark's height on every side.
- **Minimum size:** mark 16 px; full lockup 96 px wide.
- **Don't:** stretch or distort, recolour outside the palette, rotate, or add effects or busy grounds.

## Colour

| Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Radiograph Navy | `#0E2236` | Primary ink, dark grounds |
| Spark Blue | `#2D6FDB` | The spark, "AI", actions: the only accent |
| Spark Light | `#7FA9F5` | The spark on dark |
| Ice | `#EAF1FC` | Tints, surfaces |
| Paper | `#F7F9FB` | Backgrounds |
| Slate | `#5B6B78` | Secondary text |

Ratio about 60 navy/paper, 30 ice/slate, 10 spark. Use Spark Blue sparingly so it keeps signalling
"insight". Finding colours in the radiograph viewer, photos and charts are semantic, not brand colours:
they always come with a label.

## Type

Plus Jakarta Sans: ExtraBold 800 for the wordmark and headlines (−3 % tracking), Medium 500 and Regular
400 for text. JetBrains Mono for findings, measurements and labels (`#36 · CARIES · 94%`). The fonts and
their SIL Open Font Licences are self-hosted in `static/fonts/` (the app works offline). Latin subsets in
`static/fonts/web/` are embedded into SVG charts.

## Name

Written **ToothAI**, one word, capital T and capital AI. Never "Tooth-AI", "Tooth AI" or "Dent-AI".
